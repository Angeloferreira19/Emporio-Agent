# Online purchase return policy

*Illustrative English translation of a Portuguese-language interaction. Policy details are from the supplied store policy document.*

**Customer:** I bought an instrument online and changed my mind. Can I return it?

**Empório da Música:** For an online purchase, you can request a return within 7 calendar days after receiving the product, without giving a reason. It must be in its original packaging, unused, and include all accessories and manuals. The store covers the return shipping, and the refund is issued to the original payment method within 10 business days. Would you like help contacting the store?
